About

AI/ML Engineer at Amazon Web Services with over 10 years of experience in machine learning, data science, and Gen AI. Currently focused on building Gen AI applications, multi-agent system design, and agentic frameworks.

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from UC Santa Cruz, combining rigorous academic training with hands-on industry experience. I've built production ML systems at scale, published peer-reviewed research, and developed open-source tools for causal inference.

My work spans Gen AI, ML engineering, time series forecasting, and causal inference. I'm passionate about building tools that solve real problems — from forecasting models that optimize logistics to Python packages that make econometric methods accessible.
